How to read this report
01 / Time comes first
Every text and engagement snapshot has an observation time. It can only affect a forecast after it was observed. Exchange calendars define sessions and holidays; historical features use information available 15 minutes after close. A live forecast can include observations available at its stated issue cutoff.
02 / Test on the future
Expanding chronological folds keep all stocks from a session together, leave a one-session gap and purge unresolved labels. Scaling is fitted only on each training fold. Model settings are fixed in advance.
03 / Know when to abstain
Two social platforms plus news, 60 training dates and 10 prior test dates are required for a trained forecast. Otherwise the output is a clearly marked persistence fallback and a FLAT paper position.
