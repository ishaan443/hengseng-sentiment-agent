Validation and limits

Eight experimental forecasts were issued before the target close. Their prospective results are pending. The rule uses equal weights across social platforms. Exa articles are reported as coverage. These forecasts use social sentiment only.

Eight stocks have two distinct social platforms and news. Meituan and LG Chem each have one social platform. The collection contains 655 social mentions. Re-scoring all matching archived attempts yielded 705 ticker-post observations. Sentiment ranges from −1 to +1. Direction and sentiment forecasts use the experimental rule, which abstains when fewer than two sources are available. The fitted sentiment model requires 60 training dates and 10 prior out-of-sample dates.

The historical baseline uses only the preceding session’s unadjusted close on each of 49 dates per stock. The November 20 run issued its output after its target close and is excluded from forecast evaluation. The November 23 forecasts were recorded in advance.
