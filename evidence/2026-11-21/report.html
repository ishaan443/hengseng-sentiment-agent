Validation and limits

Eight stocks have two distinct social platforms and news. Meituan and LG Chem each have one social platform. The saved collection provides counts. Sentiment scores at the November 21 issuance time are unavailable in the exported data. The fitted sentiment model requires 60 training dates and 10 prior out-of-sample dates.

The historical baseline uses only the preceding session’s unadjusted close on each of 49 dates per stock. The November 20 run issued its output after its target close and is excluded from forecast evaluation. The November 23 forecasts were recorded in advance.
